How to Use This Press Kit

This document is intended for journalists, editors, researchers, and news organizations preparing coverage of CeloHT.

It provides ready-to-use factual material, organizational descriptions, media guidance, and references to official CeloHT resources.

For extended background on CeloHT's mission, governance, technology, programs, brand system, and community activities, see "MEDIA_KIT.md".

Journalists should use the most recent official documentation and confirm time-sensitive statistics, partnerships, quotes, deployments, and program status before publication.

About CeloHT

CeloHT is an open-source Haitian Web3 initiative focused on financial
inclusion, blockchain education, digital payments, and entrepreneurship
within the Celo ecosystem. Its permanent pillars are Education, Agents,
and Reforestation. CeloHT has no native token and is not an investment
product. The initiative is community-governed and its public code and
documentation are developed openly.

Wallet Compatibility

CeloHT follows a wallet-agnostic strategy.

The CeloHT dApp is designed to support multiple wallet access methods rather than requiring one specific wallet provider.

Current documented integrations include:

- MiniPay, when the dApp is opened inside MiniPay
- Valora, through WalletConnect
- Other compatible mobile wallets, through WalletConnect or another supported integration

Wallet availability and functionality may vary according to device, wallet provider, network configuration, and the current dApp implementation.

See the relevant wallet documentation for implementation-level details.

Product and Technical Status

The CeloHT dApp is deployed and testable on Celo Sepolia with core wallet and smart-contract integration implemented.

Production Mainnet rollout remains subject to the completion and verification of relevant production requirements, including Mainnet deployments, backend and indexer readiness, security review, and end-to-end validation.

Journalists should not describe a feature as fully operational in production unless its current production status has been independently confirmed.

For current technical status, consult the project's official technical repositories and documentation.

Extended:

CeloHT is an open-source Haitian Web3 initiative founded in Léogâne, Haiti, on April 16, 2022. The initiative focuses on financial inclusion, blockchain education, digital payments, and entrepreneurship within the Celo ecosystem.

CeloHT's permanent pillars are Education, Agents, and Reforestation. Its technology stack includes Celo blockchain infrastructure, USDm for supported stable-value payment flows, CELO for network-level functions such as gas, smart contracts, application infrastructure, and open-source software.

CeloHT does not issue a native token, conduct an ICO or presale, or operate an investment product. Its governance model is community-oriented, with decision-making structures documented publicly.

CeloHT's public code and documentation are developed openly under applicable open-source licenses, including Apache 2.0 where specified.

Learn more at "celoht.com" or the official "Celo-HaiTi" GitHub organization.

Media FAQs

Is CeloHT affiliated with the Celo Foundation?

CeloHT is built on the Celo blockchain but is a distinct initiative. CeloHT is not officially affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or operated by the Celo Foundation unless a formal relationship is separately and publicly confirmed.

Is CeloHT affiliated with Valora, MiniPay, or WalletConnect?

No formal affiliation should be assumed from technical integration or documentation references.

CeloHT supports compatible wallet technologies as infrastructure choices. Listing a wallet or connectivity protocol does not constitute an endorsement, sponsorship, partnership, or ownership relationship.

Which wallets does CeloHT support?

CeloHT follows a wallet-agnostic strategy.

The documented wallet integrations include MiniPay, Valora through WalletConnect, and other compatible mobile wallets through supported wallet integrations.

Does CeloHT have a token?

No. CeloHT has no native token.

Any third party claiming to sell a CeloHT token, conduct a CeloHT token presale, or offer an official CeloHT investment opportunity should not be treated as authorized by CeloHT.

Can journalists quote CeloHT statistics directly?

Only statistics confirmed by current official CeloHT documentation or directly confirmed by an authorized CeloHT representative should be presented as current facts.

Historical statistics should be clearly identified as historical.

Do not extrapolate, estimate, or combine figures from different reporting periods without confirmation.

Is the CeloHT dApp production-ready?

The dApp is deployed and testable on Celo Sepolia.

Production Mainnet readiness depends on verified Mainnet deployments, backend and indexer production readiness, security review, and end-to-end validation.

Does CeloHT operate its own blockchain?

No. CeloHT is built on the Celo blockchain and does not operate a proprietary CeloHT blockchain.

Is CeloHT an investment opportunity?

No. CeloHT is not an investment product and does not promise financial returns.

Press Coverage Guidelines

When reporting on CeloHT, journalists are asked to:

- Accurately describe CeloHT as an open-source Haitian Web3 initiative.
- Describe CeloHT as community-governed rather than founder-controlled.
- State that CeloHT has no native token.
- Avoid describing CeloHT as an investment product.
- Avoid implying that CELO or USDm are CeloHT-issued assets.
- Distinguish CeloHT from the Celo Foundation, Valora, MiniPay, WalletConnect, Opera, and other external organizations.
- Do not treat wallet integrations as evidence of formal partnerships.
- Identify whether a feature is on testnet, production Mainnet, pilot, planned, or under development.
- Attribute statistics to their source and reporting period.
- Avoid presenting illustrative, simulated, or demo data as actual user activity.
- Confirm partnership claims before publication.
- Confirm quotations with the named speaker or authorized CeloHT contact.

Citation Guidelines

When citing CeloHT in an article, report, academic publication, presentation, or other media:

- Identify CeloHT as the source organization when citing official CeloHT statements or documentation.
- Link to the official CeloHT website or relevant official GitHub repository where appropriate.
- Name the specific source document when relying on a particular policy, technical document, report, or statistic.
- Include the relevant publication or reporting date for time-sensitive information.
- Do not present historical figures as current figures without verification.
- Do not attribute statements to CeloHT representatives unless the quotation has been confirmed.
- Do not cite third-party claims as official CeloHT statements.
