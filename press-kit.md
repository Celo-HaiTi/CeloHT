# Press Kit

## For Journalists and Content Creators

This Press Kit is a concise starting point for media coverage of CeloHT. For the full media-ready resource set, including interview guidance, press release templates, attribution rules, and official contact details, see [PRESS_KIT.md](PRESS_KIT.md).

## Quick Overview

- **Name**: CeloHT
- **Founded**: April 16, 2022, in Léogâne, Haiti
- **Founder**: Johnny Dubic
- **Type**: Open-source Haitian Web3 initiative
- **Mission**: Financial inclusion, blockchain education, digital payments, and entrepreneurship within the Celo ecosystem
- **Permanent Pillars**: Education, Agents, and Reforestation
- **Governance**: Community-governed, not founder-controlled
- **Token status**: No native token; not an investment product
- **Core blockchain**: Celo
- **Wallet strategy**: Wallet-agnostic, with documented compatibility through supported wallet integrations

## Key Messages

1. CeloHT is an open-source Haitian Web3 initiative focused on practical financial inclusion and education.
2. CeloHT is built on the Celo blockchain and does not operate its own proprietary blockchain.
3. CeloHT has no native token and is not an investment product.
4. CeloHT is community-governed and should not be framed as a founder-controlled organization.
5. CeloHT supports accessible digital finance through education, agent-based services, and reforestation-linked community initiatives.
6. Technical features, program status, partnerships, and statistics should always be reported according to their current verified status.

## Media Facts

### About CeloHT

CeloHT is an open-source Haitian Web3 initiative focused on financial inclusion, blockchain education, digital payments, and entrepreneurship within the Celo ecosystem. Its permanent pillars are Education, Agents, and Reforestation.

### Important Clarifications

- CeloHT is not a cryptocurrency, token issuance project, or investment platform.
- CeloHT does not issue a native token.
- CeloHT is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or operated by the Celo Foundation unless a formal relationship is publicly confirmed.
- Wallet integrations do not automatically imply partnerships or endorsements.

## Reporting Guidance

When covering CeloHT, journalists should:

- describe it accurately as an open-source Haitian Web3 initiative
- avoid presenting CeloHT as an investment opportunity or token project
- distinguish CeloHT from external organizations such as the Celo Foundation, Valora, MiniPay, and WalletConnect
- identify whether a feature is on testnet, pilot, or production Mainnet
- attribute statistics and claims to official sources or verified documentation
